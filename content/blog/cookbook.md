## I have decided to add a cookbook section to my website

I enjoy cooking a lot, and I am the main cook in my household. I follow recipies for things that I haven't made before, but I will usually end up improvising a lot, and I will typically go off memory plus intuition for any follow up cooks. I also hate recipe sites, as they usually have too many ads, and too much waffling on. Because of that, I will be compiling all my favourite recipies here, displayed in the same style that I cook, which is very casual and feel based, rather than strict weights and volumes.
